# Resonance Reveal

## Purpose

The post-call transition shown after the user ends the voice call. Ending the call clears the active session and replaces the call route with this screen.

## Visual reference

Use the supplied Resonance Reveal screenshot and `assets/images/screen.png` as the background artwork. Keep the art full-bleed with a subtle plum tint. The header has the Resonance lock mark, “Resonance Reveal” title, and profile shortcut. Below it, show the “Synthesizing Resonance” and “99% Sync” badges. Set the serif reveal copy and body text over the lower artwork, followed by “Aligning Communication Rhythms” and the four-item bottom navigation.

## Interaction and prototype state

- The End control on the voice-call screen clears the call session and navigates here.
- The profile shortcut opens Edit Profile.
- The sync percentage and synthesis status are visual prototype values; no matching or synthesis service is connected yet.
- The bottom navigation is presentational until the Explore, Sanctuary, and Chemistry destinations are implemented.
