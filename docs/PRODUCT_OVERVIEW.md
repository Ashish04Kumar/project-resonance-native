# Product overview

Resonance is the temporary working name for an Android dating and relationship compatibility app. Natural AI voice conversations and interactive mini-games help it understand each person's relationship preferences, communication style, and values, so it can help people discover more compatible partners.

## Experience direction

The first app screen is the Resonance Welcome and Prologue screen from the Figma file. The planned first journey continues through language and cadence selection, consent and microphone setup, a guided Chemistry Call, four Chemistry Lab games, and a reviewable Connection Blueprint. The voice provider and matching details are not decided yet; see the living instructions in AGENTS.md before implementing them.
